Registration Process

Registration for the 2026–2027 season is open now. Register your team on our Current Forms page. This page describes the registration process for teams and individuals, as well as getting onboarded for Discord and Edly.

  • Team acceptances: Check that your coach/captain has returned the team acceptance form with the Team Status table below. If not, it should be in an email with the subject line CRE[AT]E Challenge acceptance + Kickoff sent on Friday October 16th (if your team registered before the kickoff).
  • Student acceptances: On the team status dashboard, check the student status. When your team coach/captain returns the team acceptance form, it triggers an email to be sent to all team members, who must then individually fill out a student acceptance form.
  • A Discord invitation is included in all acceptance confirmation emails. Please join the Discord and introduce yourself!
  • Can't find the emails above? Have you checked your junk/spam folder? Are you using a school/company email that heavily blocks/filters content?
  • Does your team not appear on the Team Status table?
    • If you registered after kickoff (October 17th), we are on a slower processing cadence for team acceptances. Please allow up to two weeks for team acceptance responses.
    • If you registered before kickoff, please contact us at bwsi-create-challenge@mit.edu.
If you already registered, please do not submit a second registration. Instead, check your spam folder(s) and if what you are looking for is not there, email us.
