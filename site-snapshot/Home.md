# Home

> **Snapshot note:** Full page content was captured on 2026-05-11 but is not reproduced here. Visit [https://beaver-works-assistive-tech.mit.edu/home](https://beaver-works-assistive-tech.mit.edu/home) for the live page.

The home/landing page of the Beaver Works Assistive Technology site. Introduces the CRE[AT]E Challenge — a year-round, asynchronous challenge for high-school and college teams to design assistive technology devices in partnership with people with disabilities. Hosted by MIT Beaver Works Summer Institute (BWSI).

Key sections on the homepage, top to bottom:
- Slim status line above the hero, stating whatever is currently in-season (right now: 2026-2027 registration open, linking to the live Formstack registration form). Update this text each time the season changes; see issue #15 for a shared, site-wide version of this component.
- Hero/value-prop: three feature cards (Online Course, Community of Learners, Expert Design Reviews)
- Social proof: three testimonial quotes from past student teams, plus co-designer story videos (Levi, Izzy, Skylar) pulled up from the About page so there's proof of impact above the fold
- Interest band reinforcing that 2026-2027 team registration is open, with CTAs to register and to the Getting Started guide
- "Get involved" callout cards: Become a Technical Mentor, Browse Past Projects, Support the Program
- Team distribution map (Leaflet, current challenge-cycle team locations)
- "Part of MIT Beaver Works" section (BWSI overview + logo)
- Navigation to key sections: Challenge, Course, Projects, Dashboard
- Social media links (YouTube, Instagram, X/Twitter)
