# Project Funding

New canonical page created for issue #18, resolving conflicting funding descriptions previously split across the Getting Started page ("grant... as part of preliminary mentor design review") and the FAQ (email-based process). The FAQ's mechanism is canonical; Getting Started and the FAQ now point here instead of restating it.

## How to request funding

Registered teams who need money to build their assistive technology (AT) product can request funding directly from Beaver Works by email:

- **To:** bwsi-create-challenge@mit.edu
- **Subject:** `TEAM NUMBER - funding request`, replacing *TEAM NUMBER* with the team's 3- or 4-digit team number
- **Cc:** all team members
- **Body:** an explanation of what the funding is needed for — the more detail, the easier it is for Beaver Works to make a determination

There is no separate funding form, and funding is not requested or granted as part of a mentor design review.

## Eligibility and amount

Teams may receive up to **$125** of funding from Beaver Works. At the moment, funding is only available to **US-based high school teams**.

## Prioritization

Funding is limited. Beaver Works uses the financial background information supplied during team registration to help prioritize requests.

## Deadline

No deadline is stated anywhere on the live site for funding requests. This is flagged as an open gap rather than an invented date — teams planning around a specific date should email to confirm timing.

## Worked example

The page includes a sample (fictional) funding request email for a team requesting ~$65 for parts for an adaptive utensil grip project, illustrating the subject line format, cc'ing teammates, and explaining the need with enough detail to be actionable.
