# Maker Skills

Module 4 of the CRE[AT]E Course. Index page for the Maker Skills section. Teams are asked to complete two of the four sections most relevant to their project.

## Introduction

Designing and fabricating assistive technology projects can involve a wide variety of skills depending on what is being built — from mechanical engineering and 3D printing to web development, electronics, and sewing. AT projects have a lot in common with the Maker Movement.

**Key instruction:** Please complete two sections that are most relevant to your project. If your project covers something not addressed in these sections, ask on the Discord for additional resources.

## Sub-pages

- [[Mechanical Making]] — machine tools, 3D printing, CAD (Onshape)
- [[Software]] — web/desktop/mobile development, GitHub, Figma, MIT App Inventor, AI/ML tools
- [[Electronics]] — circuits, microcontrollers (Arduino, Adafruit Feather, Raspberry Pi), Tinkercad simulator
- [[Wearables]] — wearable components, ergonomics, form factor, AFFOA curriculum
