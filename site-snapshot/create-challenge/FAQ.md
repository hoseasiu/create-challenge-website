## Resources for Participating — funding Q&A (issue #18)

> **Update, 2026-08-05:** The "I am on a participating team, how do I receive funding?" answer under **Resources for Participating** is being consolidated into a new canonical page, [Project Funding](https://beaver-works-assistive-tech.mit.edu/create-challenge/project-funding) (`project-funding.html` in this repo), instead of restating the mechanism here. This snapshot entry documents that change for the archive; **the live Google Sites FAQ page itself still needs a manual edit from a site admin** to replace its full answer text with a short pointer to the new page — there is no `faq.html` embed in this repo to change directly.
>
> For reference, the live FAQ answer as of 2026-08-05 read: "If your team needs funding to build your AT product, please send us an email at bwsi-create-challenge@mit.edu with the subject 'TEAM NUMBER - funding request' replacing TEAM NUMBER with your 3- or 4-digit team number, and cc all team members. Please explain what you need funding for - the more details you are able to provide, the easier it will be for us to make a determination. Teams may receive up to $125 of funding from Beaver Works, and students' financial backgrounds (as provided during registration) will be used to prioritize funding requests." (Eligibility, stated elsewhere on the same page: "At the moment, we are only able to provide grants to US-based high school teams.")
