# Design Reviews

Explains the CRE[AT]E Challenge's design review process: four required reviews per team — two peer reviews and two remote mentor reviews — plus the "Gates" that structure them through the season.

## Overview
Design reviews let a team check its ideas/prototypes against developed requirements, catch scope issues, and get pointed to resources. All four reviews are required; design reviews must be student-led (coaches should not be primary presenters).

## Peer Reviews & Remote Mentor Reviews
- **Peer Reviews** — done live (video call or in person), can include multiple teams at once, teams arrange their own (Challenge Discord #looking-for-peer-reviewers channel recommended), teams should avoid pairing only with other teams from the same school, and should have a Q&A period with a scribe capturing feedback.
- **Remote Mentor Reviews** — done asynchronously via recorded video, reviewed by mentors not already assigned to that team, matched by expertise where possible.

## Time Zones & Extensions
- All due dates are 11:59:59 PM Anywhere on Earth (AoE, UTC-12:00).
- Late submissions are allowed but come with consequences: peer reviews become harder to arrange, mentor reviews aren't guaranteed feedback, and late final-event submissions won't be considered for the final event.

## The Gates (five collapsible sections on the live page)
1. **White Gate — Preliminary Peer Review** (due Feb 1, 2026): early peer review covering co-designer intro, interview results, product objective, risks, visual representation, and a preliminary (paper/cardboard-level) prototype. Includes Presenter and Reviewer guideline checklists.
2. **Orange Gate — Preliminary Design Review** (due Feb 8, 2026): remote mentor review, similar scope to White Gate plus a bill of materials; prerequisite for up to $125 Beaver Works funding. Submitted as a YouTube video link.
3. **Blue Gate — Critical Peer Review** (due Mar 8, 2026): peer review after ~2 design iterations, expects hi-fidelity (sturdier-material) prototypes, re-introduces the co-designer, and covers resolved/remaining risk and iteration history. Includes Presenter and Reviewer guideline checklists.
4. **Brown Gate — Critical Design Review** (due Mar 15, 2026): remote mentor review, same presenter guidelines as Blue Gate, submitted as a YouTube video link.
5. **Black Gate — Final Event Submissions** (due Apr 12, 2026, no extensions): three-part submission — a written Document (co-designer profile, needs/requirements, concept generation, bill of materials, software tools, technical drawings, build instructions, LLM use disclosure, test results, reflections), a 4-5 minute Video (strict time limit, following course presentation guidelines), and a two-slide 16:9 PowerPoint Snapshot (team photo + product hero shot). Submitted via Google Form with video via Dropbox.

## Design Review FAQ
Covers: checkoffs are required (forms on Edly); not every member must present at every review; teams may do reviews early; extension consequences; finding peer-review partners via Discord; guidance on visual representations by product type (mechanical, electrical, fabric/wearable, software); handling of co-designer photos/video and the Media Release form requirement; why reviews are called "Gates" with colors (a Beaver Works AT tradition); and why "preliminary"/"critical" terminology is borrowed from government/aerospace/medical-device design review practice (PDR/CDR).
