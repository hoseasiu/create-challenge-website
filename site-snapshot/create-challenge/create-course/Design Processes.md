## Co-Designers

- Video: "Working with Co-Designers"
- **What is co-design, and who are co-designers?** — co-design gives the end user an active role throughout the process (requirements, testing, feedback, refinement); co-designers may include caretakers/family members; distinguishes co-designer from client/user/customer.
- **How do I find a co-designer?** — ability to meet regularly is key; consider disability type vs. team skills; reach out to community organizations serving people with disabilities; remember that elderly family/neighbors can be co-designers too.
- **Things to keep in mind** — design for a specific person, not an abstract category; co-designers don't have all the answers; respect privacy of personal/medical information.

## What Is Prototyping? / Prototyping in Practice

Real case study: a mobile arm support for children/adults with cerebral palsy, developed at the University of Alabama Huntsville (Randall Hajjar, Stephanie Shelton, Madison Howard, Giulia Palma, Nicolas Ward, Erin Looney), walked through as the model design process.

- Definition of "prototype."
- **Mission Statement** for the case-study team.
- **Benchmarking** — Medifab Dynamic Arm Support and Armon Edero reviewed as existing products.
- **Lead User and Experts Survey** — 8 findings from therapists/parents (safety, cleanability, comfort, weight, cost, etc.).
- **Requirements** — Physical, Functional, Performance, and Operational requirement lists; footnote link to degrees of freedom.
- **Device Concept** — product breakdown diagram (Level 1/2/3) and a labeled CAD rendering.
- **Prototyping Round 1** — cardboard/duct-tape feasibility prototype; two branching scenarios (post breaks, cuff doesn't fit all sizes) and a decision point.
- **Prototyping Round 2** — wood/metal/off-the-shelf-joint prototype with an updated rendering; a scenario surfacing 4 issues (sharp edges, weight, sticking joint, comfort) and a decision point on prioritization.
- Final delivered device photo (delivered to the United Cerebral Palsy center, with cushioned inserts).
- **Review** and **Reflections** (reflection prompt on the prototyping process).
